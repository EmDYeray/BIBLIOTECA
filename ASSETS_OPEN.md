# Integrated open-source and credited assets

The project includes three models from NASA 3D Resources. They were converted to OBJ, normalized and remapped to the native renderer's material palette:

- **Active Cavity Irradiance Monitor Satellite (AcrimSAT):** Replaces primary satellite models while retaining the panels, central body and antennas as a single stable object.
- **Viking Lander:** Replaces selected lunar or icy-world lander models with more detailed legs, platform and antenna.
- **Astronaut**, by DigitalSpace Corporation, published by NASA 3D Resources: The arms were repositioned for the lunar scene, suit colors were retained as OBJ vertex colors, and the visor has a golden finish. [Model and NASA Science credit](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/astronaut/).

Sources:

- https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
- https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/

NASA provides these 3D resources for download and use subject to its media guidelines. This project credits NASA 3D Resources and does not use NASA insignia as branding. The meshes were exported without external textures to keep the package self-contained.
